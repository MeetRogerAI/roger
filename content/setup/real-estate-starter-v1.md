-Drip campaigns: Building email and text sequences for new leads, past clients and open house visitors, so no one falls through the cracks.

-Newsletter write-ups: Timely, useful content people actually want to read, like why interest rates are on the rise, neighbourhood micro-market trends and reminders such as the Vacant Home Tax deadline, all written in the agent’s voice. Extra:
