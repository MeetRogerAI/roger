## Play 1: External messages drafted for approval
### About 1.5 hours a week saved (1.0 to 2.0)

When the member needs to write to someone, Instinct grounds the context, drafts the message in his voice, and holds it for his yes. Nothing sends without approval. This week: six drafting jobs, including notes to a university official, a mentor follow-up, and business replies.

**Playbook**
1. **Play**: External message drafted for approval. A ready draft in the member's voice, sent only on his yes.
2. **Trigger**: a request to write to someone, or a substantive reply landing on a watched thread.
3. **Setup first**: Which account sends it? What signoff and style rules apply? Any relationships with special handling?
4. **Steps**: (1) Read the actual thread and related history before drafting. (2) Draft in the member's voice with his style rules (plain words, his signoff, no dashes as punctuation). (3) Check recipient and facts against the thread, not memory. (4) Hold the draft and show it; on approval, send and verify in Sent.
5. **Guardrails**: nothing external sends without the member's yes; a quoted "yes" approves only the quoted message; held drafts stay held across reminders until he decides.
6. **Output**: the draft text plus recipient and thread, one line on what it commits him to.
7. **Pitfalls seen**: a reply sent from the wrong account once risked confusion - now the sending account is stated explicitly per draft.
8. **Done when**: the member has seen the draft and either approved, edited or killed it.
9. **Paste to install**: "Draft any external message I ask for, in my voice, and hold it for my yes before sending."

**Playbook**
1. **Play**: Inbox autoreply triage. Autoreplies filed read, substantive replies surfaced.
2. **Trigger**: an inbox event subscription on the member's connected mail.
3. **Setup first**: Which senders are always noise? Which threads are being watched for real replies?
4. **Steps**: (1) On each incoming message, classify autoreply vs substantive. (2) Autoreply on a handled thread: mark read, no ping. (3) Substantive: leave unread and report the sender, thread and what it needs. (4) When a message changes a known plan (a booking time, a deadline), say so explicitly in the report.
5. **Guardrails**: never mark a substantive reply read; never reply to anyone from triage alone; booking and deadline changes always surface even when the message itself is automated.
6. **Output**: silence for noise; one report line per substantive item.
7. **Pitfalls seen**: an automated booking confirmation looked like noise but carried a time change - now automated messages that change plans are reported.
8. **Done when**: the inbox holds no unread autoreplies and every real reply was reported.
9. **Paste to install**: "Watch my inbox: mark autoreplies read silently, and surface any substantive reply or plan change with sender and thread."
