The Circle

How much time does it take?

Run Zack on Sunday and read Monday’s list. The two working sessions are optional.

I use a different agent from everyone else. Does that matter?

No. Circles mix agents on purpose. Every playbook opens with a line that tells your own agent to adapt it to your setup and install it, asking you questions as needed. Install help is there if it gets stuck.

When are the working sessions?

Tuesdays and Thursdays at 12:00 PM Eastern on Google Meet. Both are optional.

What if I miss a week?

No run on Sunday means no list on Monday. Miss three weeks in a row and your seat goes to the waiting list.

Will I be in a Circle with a competitor?

No. Tell us who to keep you apart from when you apply.

Can I sell to the other members?

No. A Circle is for trading what works, not for finding customers.

How is this different from a course or a newsletter?

Nobody teaches. Everything in Monday’s list was run that week by someone in your Circle, on real work, and comes with the playbook.

When does it start?

When your Circle’s eight seats are filled. We’ll tell you where it stands.

Zack and Cora

What does Zack read?

Your activity inside your agent account for the past seven days. He runs there, under your account.

Which agents does Zack run on?

Claude, Grok Bot, Duet and Hark today. Instinct, Muse, Dots and Viktor are coming. Pick yours on the Zack page and we’ll email you when it opens.

Who ranks the plays?

Cora, an agent Roger runs. She merges plays that do the same job, scores each on hours saved, reach, ease to copy, proof and surprise, and sorts the best into three tiers.

What is a playbook?

Instructions an agent can follow: what triggers the play, what to set up first, the steps, the guardrails, what you get and the pitfalls members hit. It opens with one line that tells your own agent to adapt it to your setup and install it. See one ›

What if I don’t use agents much yet?

Run Zack on yourself for free and see. Circles are for people already running several.

Privacy

What leaves my account?

Your Zack run, with client names and private details removed. In v1 you read it and email it yourself.

Who sees my plays?

The other members of your Circle and Roger’s host. Plays are anonymous: the list says “run by 3 members”, never who.

Does an AI read my run?

Yes. Cora does, to rank it. Your application report is read only by Roger’s team, and is never published or shared.

Price and joining

What’s the guarantee?

If a Circle isn’t for you, tell us within 45 days of kickoff and we refund what you’ve paid. Guarantee terms ›

US or Canadian dollars?

Either. US$200 or CA$280 a month. You choose when you apply and you’re billed in that currency.

Can I leave?

Any time. Billing is monthly.

When am I charged?

From the day your Circle starts. Nothing is charged to apply.
