How it works

Your week in a Circle.

One prompt on Sunday. One ranked list on Monday. Everything else is optional.

The week, day by day

  1. SundayBy 11:59 PM Eastern

    Collect. You run Zack in your own agent account. He reads your past seven days and writes up your best plays. You read the run, then email it to hello@meetroger.ai.

  2. Monday1:00 AM Eastern

    Rank. Cora reads every run from your Circle, merges plays that do the same job, scores each one and sorts the best into three tiers.

  3. MondayMorning

    Share. Your list arrives by email: Install this week, Worth trying, Niche but great, and your own top 3.

  4. Tuesday12:00 PM Eastern · optional

    Deploy. Working session on Google Meet, one hour, hosted by Peter. Install the play you picked, with help.

  5. Thursday12:00 PM Eastern · optional

    Deploy. Second working session. Bring what broke.

  6. All week

    Use. Work the way you work. Zack only sees what you actually did.

What gets removed. Twice.

Zack strips private details before a run leaves your account. Cora checks again before anything reaches your Circle.

  • Names of anyone but you
  • Client and company names
  • Addresses, emails, phone numbers, meeting links
  • Order numbers and prices tied to a person
  • Children’s names and schools

Replaced with roles: “a buyer”, “a listing client”, “a family of five”. If a play can’t be described without a private detail, it is dropped.

The three rules

  1. 1

    Contribute.

    Send your Zack run by Sunday at 11:59 PM Eastern. No run, no list that Monday. A run that arrives late counts for the next week. Miss three weeks in a row and your seat goes to the waiting list.

  2. 2

    Confidential.

    Nothing shared in your Circle leaves it. Every member signs an agreement before joining. Plays are anonymous: the list says “run by 3 members”, never who.

  3. 3

    Candid.

    Share what failed as well as what worked. A play that wasted your week saves seven other people theirs. And no selling to each other.

The 45-day guarantee

If a Circle isn’t for you, tell us within 45 days of kickoff and we refund what you’ve paid.

  • Ask within 45 days of your Circle’s kickoff date and you get back every membership payment you’ve made.
  • One email to hello@meetroger.ai. No form and no call.
  • Refunds go to your original payment method, in the currency you paid, within 10 business days.
  • After a refund your membership ends and your seat goes to the waiting list.

After 45 days: leave any time. Billing is monthly.
